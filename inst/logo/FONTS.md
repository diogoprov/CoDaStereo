# Logo fonts

The CoDa Stereo hex logo wordmark uses two typefaces:

- **CODA** — *Ryga* (Regular). Free font, source: https://justfreefonts.com/fonts/ryga/
  Chosen for its neon-outline look, as a homage to Led Zeppelin's album *Coda*.
- **STEREO** — *Inter* (Bold), SIL Open Font License 1.1. A solid, 1980s nod to *Soda Stereo*.

`logo.svg` ships with **all text converted to outlines**, so neither font is
required to render the logo. `fonts/RygaRegular.otf` is included only to make
future edits of the wordmark reproducible.
